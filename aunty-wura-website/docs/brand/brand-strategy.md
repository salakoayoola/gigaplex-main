# Brand Strategy

## Mission

Preserve and share African languages through engaging, well-crafted learning experiences that connect learners to their heritage while exploring the natural world.

## Vision

African children fluent in their mother tongues, curious about nature, and confident in their cultural identity.

## Values

**Cultural Fluency**
Rooted in Nigerian and West African life as it's actually lived - not as a museum exhibit or a diaspora projection.

**Craft**
Every piece of content, from a worksheet to a YouTube video, is made with care. Quality signals that this work matters.

**Accessibility**
Learning should work for families without expensive materials. DIY-friendly, low-cost, available.

**Accuracy**
Scientific and linguistic content is correct. We don't simplify by being wrong.

**Warmth**
Learning happens best when you're not anxious. The tone is encouraging without being patronizing.

---

## Target Audience

### Primary: Learners (Ages 7-16)
Children learning Yoruba, whether as a heritage language or new acquisition. They're used to high-quality digital content and won't engage with boring materials just because it's "educational."

### Secondary: Parents
Urban Nigerian and diaspora parents who want their children to speak Yoruba. They're often navigating their own complicated relationship with the language and looking for resources that don't feel outdated.

### Tertiary: Educators
Teachers, tutors, and homeschool parents looking for curriculum-aligned materials they can actually use without extensive modification.

---

## Positioning

**For** Nigerian children and diaspora families  
**Who** want to learn Yoruba through engaging, well-made content  
**Aunty Wúrà** is an educational brand  
**That** makes language learning feel like time with a favorite relative  
**Unlike** dry textbooks or low-production heritage content  
**Aunty Wúrà** combines scientific illustration, warm pedagogy, and cultural authenticity

---

## Brand Personality

Five words that describe how Aunty Wúrà communicates:

1. **Warm** - Approachable and encouraging, never cold or distant
2. **Sharp** - Knows her subject deeply, explains it clearly
3. **Grounded** - Culturally specific without being exclusionary
4. **Patient** - Repeats when needed, celebrates incremental progress
5. **Contemporary** - Current, not nostalgic or stuck in the past

---

## Brand Archetype

**The Sage + The Caregiver**

Aunty Wúrà knows things and wants to share them. But unlike a pure Sage archetype, she's not detached or academic. The Caregiver element means she's invested in the learner's success and creates a safe environment for mistakes.

This is the "cool young aunt" energy - someone who has expertise and shares it generously, without making you feel small for not knowing.

## Voice & Tone

### Voice (Consistent)

| Attribute | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Register | Conversational but informed |
| Vocabulary | Clear English with Yoruba woven in naturally |
| Structure | Short sentences, active voice, concrete examples |
| Cultural anchors | Nigerian seasons, local fauna, everyday life |

### Tone (Context-Dependent)

| Context | Tone |
|---------|------|
| Teaching new concept | Patient, encouraging, uses repetition |
| Celebrating achievement | Genuine, specific ("You got the tones right!") |
| Correcting mistakes | Matter-of-fact, normalizes errors |
| Marketing/social | Slightly more playful, still warm |

### Examples

**Do:**
> "The ẹyẹlé (pigeon) is everywhere in Lagos - on your roof, at the market, probably judging your outfit. Let's learn what makes it different from the àdàbà (dove)."

**Don't:**
> "In this lesson, we will be learning about the taxonomic differences between the common pigeon and the dove, which children will find educational and fun!"

**Do:**
> "Try again - the tone on 'Ọjọ́' goes up at the end. Like you're asking a question."

**Don't:**
> "Great try! You're doing amazing! Keep going, superstar!"

## Key Messages

1. **Yoruba is worth learning properly** - not as a checkbox, but as a living language
2. **Quality content exists for heritage languages** - you don't have to settle for outdated materials  
3. **Science and language go together** - learning vocabulary through nature is effective and memorable
4. **This is for you** - wherever you are in your language journey
