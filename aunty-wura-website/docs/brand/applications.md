# Brand Applications

## Digital

### Website

**Layout Principles**
- Generous white space - content should breathe
- Warm cream (`#FAFAF7`) as primary background
- Terracotta for headers, CTAs, and emphasis
- Cards with subtle shadows and rounded corners (8-16px radius)

**Component Patterns**
- Aunty Wúrà character appears as guide in key moments (onboarding, empty states, celebrations)
- Yoruba vocabulary highlighted with Wura Gold underlines or accent backgrounds
- Botanical decorative elements in section dividers, not competing with content
- Deckled edges optional for featured content cards

**Interactive States**
- Hover: Warm Sand (`#E8B87D`) backgrounds or Terracotta darkening
- Focus: Clear outline in Deep Forest
- Active: Slight scale reduction (0.98)

### YouTube

**Thumbnails**
- Aunty Wúrà character with expressive reaction to content
- Bold Yoruba vocabulary as text overlay
- Limited palette: 2-3 colors max
- High contrast, readable at small sizes

**Channel Branding**
- Banner: Illustrated scene with character, books, Nigerian flora/fauna
- Profile image: Aunty Wúrà character portrait
- Watermark: Simple logomark, low opacity

**Video Elements**
- Lower thirds: Terracotta bar with Warm Cream text
- End screens: Deckled paper texture, clear CTAs
- Chapter markers: Consistent typography

### Social Media

**Profile**
- Aunty Wúrà character portrait (works in circular crop)
- Consistent across platforms

**Post Templates**
- Quote cards: Yoruba proverbs with English translation on Warm Cream
- Vocabulary spotlights: Word + illustration + pronunciation
- Lesson teasers: Character + topic preview
- Carousels: Consistent typography and color per slide

**Captions**
- Conversational tone matching brand voice
- Yoruba words included naturally
- Questions to encourage engagement

## Print

### Books

Refer to [art-direction/](/art-direction/) for detailed illustration specifications.

**Cover Format**
- 1:1 square with circular vignette (per book-cover.json)
- Scientific illustration style
- Title in display typography
- Deckled watercolor edge treatment

**Interior Pages**
- Generous margins for annotations
- Scientific illustrations as hero images
- Vocabulary callouts with Wura Gold accent
- Activity sections clearly delineated

### Worksheets & Fact Sheets

**Layout**
- Header with terracotta band and lesson title
- Two-column layout for efficiency
- Warm Cream background feel (or actual cream paper stock)
- Line illustrations (simpler than book style)

**Content Zones**
- Learning objectives (top)
- Core content (main area)
- Activity/practice section (distinct box)
- Space for writing/drawing

### Certificates & Achievements

- Deckled border treatment
- Wura Gold accent for celebration
- Aunty Wúrà character (celebrating pose)
- Personalizable name field
- Achievement-specific illustration

### Business Materials

**Business Cards**
- Terracotta + Deep Forest primary
- Logomark on front
- Contact on back
- Uncoated textured stock

**Letterhead**
- Subtle botanical corner element
- Warm Cream paper stock
- Minimal - content is the focus

## Merchandise

### Apparel

**T-Shirts**
- Aunty Wúrà character on warm-toned blanks (terracotta, cream, forest)
- Yoruba vocabulary designs ("Ọmọ aládé" etc.)
- Illustrations from book series
- Back prints optional for phrases/proverbs

**Quality Notes**
- Mid-weight cotton, comfortable fit
- Print quality that survives washing
- Avoid cheap promotional feel

### Learning Materials

**Notebooks**
- Yoruba prompts on covers
- Lined pages with vocabulary margin
- Warm Cream paper stock

**Flashcards**
- Scientific illustration on front
- Yoruba + English + pronunciation on back
- Rounded corners, durable stock
- Matching book series sets

**Posters**
- Scientific illustrations at display size
- Educational diagrams (anatomy, habitat)
- Frame-ready proportions

## Voice in Applications

Every application should sound like Aunty Wúrà wrote it:

| Application | Voice Example |
|-------------|---------------|
| Website CTA | "Start learning" not "Begin your journey" |
| Error message | "That page wandered off. Let's find what you need." |
| Email subject | "Your àwọn ẹranko (animals) vocabulary pack is ready" |
| Social post | "Quick - what's the Yoruba word for 'elephant'? (Hint: it starts with E...)" |
| Certificate | "Confirmed: You know your ẹyẹ (birds)." |
